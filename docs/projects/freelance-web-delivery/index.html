Client Delivery and Technical Consulting

A long-running foundation in client delivery across websites, applications, integrations, and practical technical problem solving.

Digital delivery foundation 2015-Present Ongoing

Over the course of my freelance work, I built and launched more than 100 websites and web applications for small businesses and organizations.

That body of work matters less as a brand label now and more as the foundation for how I operate. It trained me to move from ambiguity to delivery quickly, and to make technical decisions in contexts where time, clarity, and trust all mattered.

The work covered a wide spread of technical and practical requirements:

  • React frontends
  • Python-backed workflows
  • WordPress builds and CMS implementations
  • custom integrations
  • maintenance and production troubleshooting

The important part was never just writing code. It was delivering something that worked in the environment the client actually lived in.

That usually meant translating vague business requests into clear software decisions, choosing tools that matched the real complexity of the project, and shipping on timelines that did not leave room for overengineering.

That experience shaped a lot of how I think about management and AI-enabled execution now:

  • solve the real bottleneck, not the fashionable one
  • handoff and documentation matter
  • maintainability matters
  • communication matters just as much as implementation

Client delivery also taught me to move comfortably between product thinking, debugging, implementation, support, and stakeholder management. In practice, those are rarely separate jobs.
