The best publishing workflow is usually the one that creates the least ceremony between having an idea and shipping it.

For a personal blog, that often means:

1. capture fragments quickly
2. shape them into a short argument
3. publish before the idea goes stale

Markdown-based content keeps that loop short. A post is just a file. Metadata is explicit. The archive stays portable.

That portability matters. Personal sites last longer when the content model is boring enough to survive redesigns, framework shifts, and hosting changes.
