Most personal sites collapse into one of two problems: they are either abandoned brochures or endlessly unfinished experiments.

The useful middle ground is a site that can hold three things at once:

- a clear introduction
- selected proof of work
- ongoing writing that shows how you think

That structure matters because it gives the site a reason to stay current. Projects change. Interests shift. Writing creates a low-friction way to keep publishing without inventing a new portfolio piece every month.

Astro is a strong fit for that model because content can stay close to the code, the build stays lightweight, and the output is simple static HTML that deploys cleanly.
