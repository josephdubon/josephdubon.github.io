A homepage does not need to tell the whole story. It needs to orient the right visitor quickly.

For a personal portfolio, the front page usually has four jobs:

- identify who you are
- show what kind of work you do
- point to proof
- offer a clear next step

Everything else can live one click deeper.

That restraint is useful because it leaves room for stronger writing, better case studies, and less visual noise. The homepage becomes an index instead of a dumping ground.
