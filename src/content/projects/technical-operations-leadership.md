A lot of my value comes from working at the boundary between software and operations.

At Right At School, I manage operations across 11 sites serving more than 500 students daily while leading a 32-person staff across multiple locations.

That kind of environment forces clarity. Problems are not theoretical. Reliability matters every day. Communication gaps become operational failures very quickly.

The work has included:

- building reporting and tracking systems
- improving accountability across sites
- standardizing day-to-day execution
- resolving issues quickly across staff, schools, and families

This is not separate from engineering thinking. It is engineering thinking applied to real-world systems made of people, schedules, constraints, and feedback loops.

Leading in those environments has made me better at identifying bottlenecks, designing cleaner workflows, and creating systems that help people perform consistently under pressure.

It is also one reason I care so much about technology education. Systems literacy, troubleshooting, and guided problem solving matter because they shape how people operate long before they ever sit in front of a production codebase.
